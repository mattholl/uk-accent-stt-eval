Can you still rustle up a Huddersfield dialect? Huddersfield's got a very particular sound to it, well every Christmas my mother's sister, my Auntie Annie used to recite a poem: I was sitting by t'hearth last evening, my mother and father were off, because we'd heard that my old non Sussanah were laid up in bed with a cough, she's some brass has me old non Sussanah that's why she's looked after so oft, if they've nowt, well then they're nobbut a bother, for example me old uncle Joe.
